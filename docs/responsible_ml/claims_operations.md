# Responsible use in claims operations

Forecast intervals are uncertain, especially during rare catastrophes and structural changes. A
calibrated P90 is not a guarantee. Under-allocation can affect regions unevenly, while conservative
staffing can impose travel and overtime burdens. Leaders must review local conditions, employee safety,
labor agreements, special skills, vendor capacity and emergent catastrophe intelligence before acting.

The model explains associations rather than causes. Synthetic workload and service penalties are not
carrier standards. Human override, audit logs, regional performance monitoring, fallback static plans,
and incident procedures are required. The platform is decision support, not an autonomous dispatcher.
